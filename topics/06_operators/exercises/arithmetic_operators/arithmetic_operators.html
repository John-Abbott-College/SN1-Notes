2. Egg Feast

Given a required number of eggs (num_eggs), where eggs can only be purchased in containers of 30, 12, 6, or as individual eggs, determine the number of containers of each size needed to purchase exactly the required number of eggs with no waste.

For example, if I need 24 eggs, I should purchase 2 containers of 12 rather than 1 container of 30.

Complete the following code:
👀 Hint 1

Start by figuring out how many of the largest containers you can buy without exceeding the number of eggs you need.

👀 Hint 2

Calculate how many eggs are still needed after buying the largest containers, then repeat the process with the next container size.

TipSolution 
num_eggs = 100
large_size = 30
normal_size = 12
small_size = 6

# 1. Finding how many large can fit in num_eggs
large_count = num_eggs // large_size

# 2. How many eggs are left after buying the large containers
remaining = num_eggs % large_size


normal_count = remaining // normal_size
remaining_n = remaining % normal_size

small_count = remaining_n // small_size

# What's left are individual eggs to purchase 
individual_count = remaining_n % small_size

print("Here's what you need to purchase...")
print("Count large: ", large_count)
print("Count normal: ", normal_count)
print("Count small: ", small_count)
print("Individual eggs: ", individual_count)
Here's what you need to purchase...
Count large:  3
Count normal:  0
Count small:  1
Individual eggs:  4
